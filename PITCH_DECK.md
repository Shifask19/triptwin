## SLIDE 4 — THE SOLUTION

# TripTwin
## An Intelligent Travel Decision Engine

TripTwin creates a **digital "Travel Twin"** for every traveler — a continuously learning AI profile that monitors, understands, and optimises your trip in real time.

### Three core innovations:

---

### 🔄 TripSwap — The Central Innovation

The system continuously monitors:
- Live weather conditions
- Real-time crowd levels
- Attraction closures and delays
- Traffic and travel time
- Your remaining budget
- Your current location

When your plan becomes suboptimal, TripTwin doesn't just alert you.

**It finds a better alternative and presents a structured decision:**

```
CURRENT PLAN             →    BETTER OPTION
────────────────────          ──────────────────────
Outdoor Sightseeing           Indoor Cultural Museum
Cost: $30                     Cost: $18
Crowd: Very High              Crowd: Low
Weather: Poor                 Weather: Excellent
Travel: 45 min                Travel: 15 min

POTENTIAL BENEFIT: Save $12 · Save 30 min · Avoid rain · Fewer crowds

[ Accept Swap ]  [ Keep Original ]  [ Show Alternatives ]
```

---

### 🧠 Travel Twin — Your Digital Travel Profile

Not a questionnaire. A continuously learning AI model built from:

- Activity choices and skips
- Time spent at each location
- Spending patterns vs budget
- Ratings and reactions
- Crowd and weather tolerance
- Food and culture preferences

The longer you use TripTwin, the more accurate it becomes.

**Goal: The app already knows you. You never explain your preferences again.**

## APPENDIX B — Key Metrics Definitions

- **MAU** — Monthly Active Users (opened app at least once in 30 days)
- **Paid Conversion** — Free users who upgrade to Explorer or higher
- **CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost (total marketing spend / new paid users)
- **LTV** — Lifetime Value (avg monthly revenue × avg months retained)
- **NPS** — Net Promoter Score (target: >60 for travel apps)
- **TripSwap Adoption** — % of active users who accept at least one swap per trip
